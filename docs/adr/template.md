# ADR NNNN — Title in sentence case

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Refs:** M<n>, #<issue>, spec §<n>

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the ambiguity, or the thing that could
not be built until this was answered — written so a reader who was not there can
reconstruct the pressure without asking anyone.

Name the options that were genuinely on the table and what was wrong with each.
An ADR whose context admits only one possible answer is recording a fact, not a
decision.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and specifically enough to be checked
against the code.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes expensive, and what it forecloses. Include
the costs — an ADR listing only benefits is advocacy, and the next reader needs
to know what was traded away in order to judge whether the trade still holds.
